# Product and rules, defined before implementation
Primary user: service-center clerk. Promise: explicit recipient confirmation and attributable custody from intake to pickup. Demo organization maple-demo; display timezone America/New_York. Local trusted workstation only.
P0: customer/mailbox/alias maintenance; image/manual/barcode intake; evidence-based suggestions and explicit confirmation; exception review; duplicate review; bilingual notification drafts and deterministic attempts; search, storage, pickup and separate authorization; immutable history, corrections and CSV.
Parking lot: billing, real OCR provider, live delivery, production identity/MFA, external identity verification and postal certification.
Entities: Customer has Mailbox and CustomerAlias; MailItem references recipient, StorageLocation and LabelImage; OCRRun preserves source suggestions; MatchCandidate records evidence; IntakeConfirmation records clerk choice; PickupAuthorization independently records third party and staff check; CustodyEvent and AuditEvent append only; NotificationDraft has DeliveryAttempt with stable unique key.
State transitions: received → available/exception/returned; available → collected/returned/exception; exception → available/returned. Terminal collected and returned cannot transition. Corrections append history and regenerate unsent drafts. SQLite immediate transactions serialize collection. All queries use fixed demo organization; request IDs cannot select another business.
Staff access for synthetic local demo: Alex Rivera (clerk) can intake, collect and simulate notification attempts; Morgan Lee (manager) can additionally change customer and custody records, create authorizations, export, change retention and reset; Casey Chen (auditor) has read-only access and may export. Each session uses a server-generated credential, HttpOnly cookie, CSRF token and eight-hour expiry. Authentication and authorization are enforced at the HTTP boundary, not only in the browser; staff actor context populates audit history. Production identity remains out of scope.
Acceptance scenarios: anonymous cannot read records or uploaded images; clerk cannot alter records outside their role; auditor cannot mutate; missing CSRF blocks write; distinct staff produce distinct custody attribution; logout revokes session; manual intake persists; Maria Garcia yields two candidates and requires evidence; inactive recipient rejected; duplicate available tracking requires reason; concurrent pickups produce one event; correction retains history; wrong recipient notifications blocked; expired labels deleted with audit.
